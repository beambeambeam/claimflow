# Finance Officer reviews the Department Head, and that approval is final

Every claim needs an assigned reviewer, and reviewers follow the reporting line. The Department Head is at the top of that line, so we make the Finance Officer the Department Head's assigned reviewer from Sprint 1. When the assigned reviewer is a Finance Officer, their approval is final, even for a high-value claim. The second stage exists to get Finance to look at large claims, and in this case Finance has already looked. With a single Finance Officer, a second stage would also mean the same person deciding the same claim twice.

**Considered Options**

- Block the Department Head from submitting until Sprint 3. Rejected: it leaves a real member unable to claim, and the team wanted the full reporting line from the start.
- Require a different Finance Officer for the final decision. Rejected: it needs at least two Finance Officers and an extra "different person" rule for little benefit.

**Consequences**

- Sprint 1 has three roles. The Finance Officer only acts as an assigned reviewer until final decisions are added in Sprint 3.
- Finance Officers have no assigned reviewer, so they cannot create claims. The general rule "no assigned reviewer, no claims" covers this; there is no special case.
