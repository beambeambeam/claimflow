\subsection*{Demo 2: rule rejection under the field}

\demofacts
 {F-02; F02-AC-06, F02-AC-07, F02-AC-11; BR-07, BR-12; F02-TEST-12, F02-TEST-17}
 {Requester \texttt{req.anan}}
 {Logged in; New Claim form (\path{/claims/new}) with a Travel item and a Meals item}
 {Enter a Meals item of 1,600.00 THB dated tomorrow; press Submit. Then set the date to yesterday and the amount to 1,000.00 and submit again.}
 {The first submit is refused with 422; both messages appear under their fields and the input is kept: ``Expense date cannot be in the future.'' (BR-07) and the Meals limit message (BR-12, up to 1,500.00). The corrected claim is accepted with a claim number and the status Submitted.}
 {As expected. The first submit was refused and both messages appeared under their fields with the input kept: ``Expense date cannot be in the future.'' and ``A Meals item cannot be more than 1,500.00 THB.'' The corrected claim was accepted as \texttt{CLM-2026-000006}, Submitted, total 1,250.00 THB.}
 {The same two rules are asserted through \path{POST /claims} by F02-TEST-17 (one 422 listing both violations) and by the table tests in F.3 (F02-TEST-09, F02-TEST-11), which pass at \texttt{ac5bce5}.}

\subsection*{Demo 3: login, logout, and a forbidden operation}

\demofacts
 {F-01, F-02; F01-AC-07, F01-AC-18, F01-AC-11, F01-AC-12; F02-AC-09, F02-AC-14; BR-15; F01-TEST-08, F01-TEST-12, F01-TEST-13, F02-TEST-14}
 {Requester \texttt{req.anan} (logout, anonymous visit); Finance Officer \texttt{fin.wanida} (forbidden operation)}
 {Seeded database, API and web running}
 {Log out as \texttt{req.anan}; open \path{/claims} without a session; log in as \texttt{fin.wanida} and open \path{/claims/new}.}
 {Logout shows the Login screen; the anonymous visit goes to \texttt{/login?redirect=\%2Fclaims}; the Finance Officer sees ``You can't create claims.'' and no form; \path{POST /claims} by that account returns 403 and stores nothing.}
 {As expected. Log out showed the Login screen; the anonymous visit to \path{/claims} showed the Login screen; \texttt{fin.wanida} on \path{/claims/new} saw ``You can't create claims.'' and no form. The API refusal of \path{POST /claims} for that account is F02-TEST-14, which passes at \texttt{ac5bce5}.}
