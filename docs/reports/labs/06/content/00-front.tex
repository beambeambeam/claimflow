\noindent\textbf{How to read this PDF.}
ClaimFlow plays two roles in Lab 6.
As the \emph{Product Team} it wrote the UAT cases (Part A), received and triaged the findings (Part C), and fixed and released them (Part D).
As the \emph{UAT Team} it tested the Queue team's Sprint 1 release (Part B).
Evidence has an ID (EB-, EC-, ED-) in its caption, with the action, the expected and actual result, the time, and the commit or revision.
Times are UTC unless marked UTC+7 (Bangkok).
The app shows amounts with the baht sign; this PDF writes it as THB, because the report fonts have no baht glyph.
